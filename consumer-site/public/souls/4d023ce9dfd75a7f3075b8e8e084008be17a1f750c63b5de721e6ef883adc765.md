## Contributions

### Original Frameworks & Mental Models

1. **The 9 Forms of Capital Applied to Bitcoin + Permaculture** — His most significant intellectual contribution. Originally the permaculture "8 forms of capital" (financial, material, living/natural, intellectual, experiential, social, cultural, spiritual), he expanded it to **9 forms** by adding **"Vital capital"** (mental and physical well-being). Organized into three "super forms": **Physical capital** (financial, material, natural), **Personal capital** (intellectual, experiential, vital), and **Relational capital** (social, cultural, spiritual). Key insight: "By spending time producing ones own food many forms of capital are accrued simultaneously. Grazing sheep, produces meat, builds soil, enhances ones vitality." Uses as time management and decision-making tool: "The name of the game is spending time efficiently, so the goal is to build multiple forms of capital simultaneously with each expenditure of time." Contrast: fiat "has eroded the value of living capital, removing the ability for poor people to live subsistence lifestyles." "Bitcoin stack will probably always be getting smaller even though purchasing power is going up because the sats will be liquidated for other forms of capital."

2. **"Permanent money; permanent culture"** — Signature philosophical formulation linking Bitcoin's monetary permanence to lasting culture. Used as presentation title and personal mantra. "We will build a permanent culture on the foundation of Bitcoin."

3. **Bitcoin Mining as "Transaction Fee Insurance"** — Owning miners at home is like buying insurance against rising transaction fees; as fees go up, miners yield more sats for the same work providing heat.

4. **"Double Spend Energy" / "Stacking Functions"** in mining — Getting multiple productive outputs from a single energy input. First is the trickle of KYC-free sats, second is heat speeding up production, third is the product to be sold for sats.

5. **The Value Chain (#valuechain)** — High-velocity V4V transactions (boosts/zaps) create cascading capital formation: "High velocity exchange builds capital, especially if that exchange causes a chain reaction of capital to propagate. Not just financial capital (btc) but also intellectual, experiential, social, cultural, spiritual, etc."

6. **Bitcoin's Successional Nature** — Drawing from ecological succession, Bitcoin's use cases "will always be in flux and that many haven't been considered or utilized yet." Bitcoin as a high-fidelity record-keeping system, not just money.

7. **Bitcoin as Cultural Development Foundation** — Bitcoin meetups are "the foundation of the bitcoin circular economies." "The Bitcoin Meetup Is the Farmers Market" — coined and demonstrated concept where meetups function as local marketplaces for circular economies.

8. **Non-Extraction as the Third Ethic of Permaculture** — Trading rabbits for ducks with a neighbor keeps energy in the local system rather than extracting value into the fiat economy.

9. **"Museum of Satoshi"** — Speculative idea that people might make ceremonial transactions (births, marriages, deaths) by burning Bitcoin to the Genesis address, creating a permanent informational library on-chain.

10. **"Meshtadel"** — Concept of interconnected Bitcoin homesteads/citadels forming a network economy. "Each citadel is the start of a meshtadel."

11. **Pioneer vs. Pleb Framework** — "Pioneers" actively build Bitcoin culture (attending meetups, starting podcasts); "plebs" still consuming slop. "Plebs become pioneers by participating in their local Bitcoin culture."

12. **Pricing in Sats vs Fiat** — "Pricing in sats works well for sales in fixed windows of time" (event tickets, weekend markets). For ongoing sales, fiat pricing with Lightning conversion is more practical.

13. **Bitcoin mining as the "keel" of a financial portfolio** — Uses sailboat metaphor: a heavy weight deep in the water providing stability.

14. **"Hash-vide" cooking** — Portmanteau of hash + sous-vide for cooking with Bitcoin mining immersion oil.
