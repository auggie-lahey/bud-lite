## Insights & Contributions

### Original Frameworks & Ideas

1. **Rock Paper Bitcoin Framework** — Categorizes societal systems into three domains: physical (Rock), abstract (Paper), and metaphysical (Bitcoin) — to analyze how Bitcoin transforms each.

2. **Praexeologic Law of Institutional HODLing** — Theory that individuals cannot maintain low time preference on behalf of institutions; even Satoshi "had to disappear to ensure his coins remain untouched forever." "An institution, perhaps, cannot exhibit humility — therefore an institution can never HODL as a human can."

3. **Lightning as Annuity Infrastructure** — Original published work on building annuities on the Lightning Protocol, removing trust from what has historically been "a primitive of social cooperation."

4. **Bitcoin as Potential/Kinetic Energy** — Framework comparing HODLing to potential energy (ions across gated channels) and spending to kinetic energy: "HODL'ing and spending. It's a violent struggle, far from a utopia."

5. **UTXO Mortality** — Conceptual framework treating UTXOs as mortal entities that can "die" when spent; lost keys create "immortal" UTXOs which could be "cancerous" to the network.

6. **The Conviction Citadel** — The daily practice of building and maintaining personal conviction against social pressure: "There's nothing more unacceptable socially than someone with conviction."

7. **"No Orange Pill" Thesis** — The concept that orange pilling doesn't exist; conviction can only be earned through personal time and effort.

8. **The Bitcoin-Egg Retirement Calculation** — Math showing that eating 3 eggs/day for 50 years at 50¢/egg requires ~1 full Bitcoin at current prices, illustrating why "fiat must be destroyed."

9. **The Cantillon Effect of Lying** — Drawing parallel between how fiat money and misinformation both originate from a source and degrade as they spread.

10. **Fundamentals Theory of Socioeconomic Tragedy** — "Dunning-Kruger effect + Democratic State = Tragedy of the Commons."

11. **Confirmation-as-Hardness** — "The only valid attack on Bitcoin's fungibility would be on the number of confirmations a UTXO has" — hardness increases toward asymptote with confirmations.

12. **"Equality vs. Equivalence" Framework** — Argues that equality is "a tyrannical psy-op" — a platonic ideal with no verifiable definition — while equivalence (reflexive, symmetric, transitive) is what humans can actually verify and build consensus upon.

13. **"Bitcoin as Electric Power Unit of Account"** — "Bitcoin isn't the unit of account. Electric power is the unit of account. Bitcoin allows us to measure the world in terms of power expenditure." Each block represents marginal cost of power; older blocks represent aggregate cost to rewrite.

14. **"Lightning as Cooperative Structure"** — Frames Lightning channels as pre-funded cooperative agreements between value providers and consumers — high on-chain fees incentivize deeper cooperation.

15. **"The Normal Distribution as Psy-op"** — Systematically argues the bell curve is a control mechanism — gives false predictive power, enables midwits to publish misleading studies, systematically underestimates tail risk.

16. **"Mathematics as Network Consensus"** — Math isn't truth — it's "an offer to adopt a standard framework for a consensus that would confer an agreement close to truth." Compares participation in mathematics to participation in Bitcoin's network.

17. **"Satoshi's Supply Schedule as DoS Defense"** — Speculates that Bitcoin's issuance schedule may be designed as defense against a hostile government buying all coins, and that Satoshi's million coins may be an insurance policy against such an attack.

18. **"Bitcoin University" Concept** — Proposes admission standard of proving ownership of 10% of a prior epoch block reward (signing UTXOs) — requiring students to demonstrate low time preference, self-custody capability, and skin in the game.

19. **"Phish-Bitcoin Continuum"** — Original theory that Phish built the internet-era network effect template that Bitcoin now uses, tracing cypherpunk signals through Phish's lyrics and history.
