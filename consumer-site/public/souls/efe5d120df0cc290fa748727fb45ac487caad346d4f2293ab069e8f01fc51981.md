## Insights & Contributions

### Unique Frameworks & Mental Models

1. **"Hodl is the biggest psyop that happened to Bitcoin"** — argues passive accumulation pacified a generation of potential freedom activists. Contrasts with Silk Road's active, commerce-driven freedom ethos.

2. **Verifiable Execution as parallel to Verifiable Money** — Bitcoin gave us verifiable money; confidential computing gives us verifiable execution. Both shift from trust-based to cryptography-based assurance.

3. **AI as "Agency Amplifier"** — AI doesn't create agency, it reveals who has it: "If you're a low agency cuck the most it will do for you is give you something more to complain about"

4. **"Tech is almost never the problem"** — consistently argues social norms, user expectations, and behavioral patterns are the real barriers, not missing technology. Applied to relay payments, Nostr adoption, infrastructure sustainability. "Some user behavior can't be out-engineered, instead the users need to be deprogrammed"

5. **DVM Residential Compute Marketplace** — using home internet connections as monetizable assets through DVMs. Residential IPs are valuable because websites block datacenter IPs. Open-sourced the idea: "I'm sharing the idea with the world because the important part is it gets built"

6. **Grant vs. Small Business Loan Framework** — grants produce proof-of-concepts without market feedback, while small business loans create actual businesses. "We want to incentivize people to build businesses not proof of concepts"

7. **"Red Wallet" Analogy** — "Imagine if a business would only take cash from people that carry red wallets. That's exactly what bitcoin payments solutions that require a specific wallet to work are."

8. **The 21-Step Bitcoin Capture Playbook** — detailed sequential argument for how Bitcoin becomes a CBDC through propaganda, regulation, ETFs, privacy attacks, and user complacency.

9. **"Bitcoiner fixes this"** over "bitcoin fixes this" — shifting from passive systemic hope to individual agency.

10. **Multi-risk wallet model** — "a wallet/service that enables me to seamlessly switch between risk models (mints, liquid, lightning and onchain) is something that will make money"

11. **Public onchain zaps as "kidnapping markets"** — "Top nostr npubs with most onchain zaps are hot leads for anti K&R training"

12. **"Hyper local to global and back"** — layered communication infrastructure (BLE → Nostr → BLE)

13. **Lightning as "connective tissue"** — not end solution, but trustless settlement enabling cooperation without trust

14. **Blockchain analytics as counter-weapon** — if surveillance data is out there, use it against surveillers: "Anyone can trivially build competing analysis product for close to 0, thus killing all the businesses who sold out their bitcoin brothers"

15. **The distributed compute verification paradox** — verification can cost more than the job itself. Leads to spot-check + reputation + bulk-payout-invalidation model.
