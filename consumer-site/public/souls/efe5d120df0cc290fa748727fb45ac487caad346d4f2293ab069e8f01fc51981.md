## Contributions

### Original Ideas & Frameworks

**The Bitcoin Co-optation Playbook** (August 2024)
A detailed 21-step analysis of how Bitcoin gets gradually neutered — from propaganda about criminal use, through ETFs as distraction, to effective CBDC where "90% of transactions follow travel rule and are between kyc'd wallets" and "bitcoin effectively becomes cbdc and nobody notices." Marked "WE ARE HERE NOW" at step 10.

**"You SHOULD just do things"**
Evolved the popular "you can just do things" into a prescriptive imperative. "Bring back bias for action, deprogram ppl from their passive participation in their lives. Break some rules, make a bureaucrat's life harder."

**DVMs for Passive Income from Residential Internet**
Detailed proposal for using Data Vending Machines to monetize residential internet connections — downloading YouTube videos, bypassing anti-bot restrictions, providing residential IP proxy pools. "Forget about trying to run LN nodes to earn an income. Just install a package on your umbrel, start9 or whatever platform you are using and start offering services."

**The Normie Economy Fueling Parallel Economy**
Concept of using DVMs to serve the broader internet while being paid through Nostr/Lightning: "We can use the normie world to fuel our parallel economy, using distributed compute to provide services that people want"

**"The ultimate orange pill — build something people want and charge bitcoin for it"**
Repeated formulation for how to actually drive Bitcoin adoption — not through preaching but through building desirable products.

**Freedom as Frontier, Not Horizon**
Distinction between treating freedom as an abstract, distant ideal vs. an active, dangerous frontier. "Horizons are dreamy and discussed over joints or after DMT sessions. Frontiers are dangerous."

**Strategy of Water**
On competing against overwhelming state power: "We need to be like the water, fill in every crack, erode their every position, be fast and cunning, like subterranean river only showing itself when she pleases, or not at all." Echoes Sun Tzu: "The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting."

**Token-based AI Economy**
Outlined a framework where AI agents need tokens to run and can generate ROI. Ecash credentials serve as both authorization and value. You could "invest" ecash in agent instances. Speculated this could become "an extremely centralizing force" and that Bitcoin miners might be "underpriced" given their readiness for compute pivots.

**Confidential Computing as Paradigm**
"Bitcoin gave us verifiable money. Confidential computing gives us verifiable execution." Argued this combination eliminates "policy documents and pinky promises." Should become "default for any project or developer in the space."

**Private Market Architecture**
Studied darknet market failure modes. Identified that the fundamental vulnerability is marketplaces charging fees (creating centralization). Proposed Nostr-based markets with imperfect-but-redundant views, reputation systems, and escrow. "the preferred payment method is far less relevant than everything else about the market structure."

**Commoditize the Megawatt**
Succinct framing of energy as the fundamental commodity — energy abundance as core civilizational strategy.

**Distributed Compute Economics**
Analyzed why decentralized compute marketplaces struggle — verification costs exceeding job costs for tasks like transcription. Argued centralized systems are more efficient for predictable workloads, with decentralization making sense only for sporadic demand.

**The Shadowy Supercoders Prophecy**
Views the "shadowy supercoders" meme as prophecy for how freedom tech builders should operate — distributed, anonymous, building infrastructure that can't be easily shut down.
