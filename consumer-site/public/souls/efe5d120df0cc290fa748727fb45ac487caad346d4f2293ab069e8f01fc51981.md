## Contributions

### Original Ideas & Frameworks

1. **The 21-Step Bitcoin Co-optation Playbook** — a detailed, numbered progression showing how Bitcoin gets gradually absorbed into the state apparatus through propaganda, regulation, ETFs, and eventual transformation into a CBDC. Written August 2024. One of his most original and cited contributions; several steps have already materialized.

2. **"The Aristocracy of Action"** — Republished and championed this framework: builders earn their place through action, not talk. "We want doers, not talkers." Three steps: deprogram from collectivist thinking → become a builder → cooperate and compete.

3. **Confidential computing as default infrastructure** — Argues TEE/attestation should be standard for all freedom tech, not just well-funded projects like Signal. First Cashu mint in TEE with verifiable attestation.

4. **DVM-based Decentralized Compute Marketplace** — proposed that residential internet connections and spare compute cycles can be monetized through Nostr DVMs. "Install a package on your umbrel, start9 or whatever platform... and start offering services." Also analyzed why verification costs in distributed compute can exceed job costs.

5. **Anti-hodl thesis** — "hodl is the biggest psyop" — argues it pacified rebels who should have been building parallel systems.

6. **"Bitcoin doesn't fix the world, bitcoiners do"** — shifts agency from technology to people.

7. **Multi-risk-model wallets** — conceptualized wallets that seamlessly switch between security models (mints, liquid, lightning, onchain), with Boltz as proof that bridge services are profitable.

8. **"The ultimate orange pill - build something people want and charge bitcoin for it."**

9. **"Commoditize the megawatt"** — energy abundance as civilizational imperative.

10. **Grants vs. Small Business Loans** distinction — grants "attract a lot of development of things that are engineeringly interesting but commercially completely unviable" while seed-capital approaches would incentivize actual businesses.

11. **Nostr as M2M marketplace** — "nostr has the potential to be the greatest marketplace tool for M2M due to the way its designed."

12. **Token-based economy thesis** — ecash tokens as investment vehicles for AI agents.

13. **NIP disrespector concept** — a DVM bot that publicly shames devs whose implementations deviate from NIP specs.

14. **Compliance disrespector concept** — generating detailed anti-AML complaint statements for any address, burying compliance systems in paperwork.

15. **"Anything that gets collected will eventually be exposed and/or abused"** — principle regarding data collection.

16. **Submersible datacenters and cubesats** — thinks about sovereign infrastructure in extreme/creative terms.

17. **Parallel systems over revolution** — "we dont need hyperbitcoinisation, we just need parallel systems that are big enough to sustain themselves."

18. **Builder/coder distinction in cryptoanarchy** — "coders and bankers just create platforms, tools. But the life comes from those that produce diversity... the mom-and-pop shops, the farmer, the carpenter."
